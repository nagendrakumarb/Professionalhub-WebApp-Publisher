Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Professional Hub ("we," "our," or "us") runs resume and exam features in the browser. This policy describes what the current browser-based application stores and what information may be sent to external providers when you choose those features. It is a product description and should be reviewed against the hosting, analytics, and provider setup used for each release.

1. Client-Side Resume Processing

Resume files and pasted job descriptions are parsed and scored in your browser. The app does not upload the resume for ATS analysis. If you use an external job-search provider, your selected keywords, location, and related search parameters are sent from the browser to that provider. Google sign-in sends authentication information to Google. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Browser Storage & Local Data Retention

Sign-in state, candidate profile details and photograph, exam attempts, certificates, job records, and preferences may be kept in browser storage such as IndexedDB and local storage. If you map a folder, selected personal settings and encrypted records may also be written to that folder. These records are not synchronized to a Professional Hub server in the current build. Clearing browser site data can remove records or make locally encrypted files unreadable in this browser profile. Export a backup before clearing data.

3. External Job Searches & Import Links

When you search or import a posting, the browser may connect directly to selected job providers or the job-posting website. Providers may receive your query and technical connection metadata, such as IP address and browser details, and handle them under their own policies. API keys entered for optional providers are stored in your browser’s mapped-folder settings using the application’s encryption, but the key must be available to the browser to call that provider.

4. Advertising and Cookies

The current app does not include an advertising-cookie consent banner. Essential browser storage supports sign-in, offline resources, preferences, and locally saved records. Google and external providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with them. See the Cookie & Browser Storage Policy.

5. Sensitive Information

A profile can include your name, email, phone, address, and photograph for exam and certificate display. Do not enter sensitive identifiers that the app does not need. If you choose Email result, the app prepares an encrypted result file and opens your email application; you decide whether to send it, and your email provider then handles the message and attachment.

6. Policy Updates & Inquiries

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any adjustments will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date. Technical questions or issue reports can be raised via our official GitHub Repository Tracker.
