Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Professional Hub is a browser-based application. It does not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers. The sign-in flow and external job providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their policies.

Storage used by Professional Hub

The app may use essential browser storage such as IndexedDB, local storage, and cache storage to keep your sign-in session, mapped-folder access reference, preferences, exam records, certificates, and offline site resources. These are browser storage mechanisms; they are not necessarily HTTP cookies.

Managing stored data

You can remove site data through your browser’s privacy settings. Doing so may sign you out and delete data saved only in that browser. Export a backup first if you need to keep local exam records or preferences. Revoking a mapped-folder permission is managed through your browser’s site permissions.

Third-party services

Google sign-in and selected job-search providers may set or read their own cookies and receive technical connection data when you use those features. Review the relevant provider’s privacy and cookie information for details.
